Taxi Rush

How to Play Taxi Rush

You are a taxi driver navigating through busy city streets, responsible for safely delivering passengers to their destinations. Doing so earns you money to upgrade your vehicle or purchase brand-new cars!

Controls & Gameplay

The controls are simple: use W, A, D or the Up, Left, Right Arrow Keys to steer and drive your taxi cab.

Give it your all to climb the leaderboard! If you need help getting started, feel free to check out the video walkthrough at the bottom of the page.
