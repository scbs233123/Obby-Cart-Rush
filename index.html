Obby Cart Rush

How to Play Obby Cart Rush

Control your minecart along thrilling obstacle tracks! Use your Arrow keys or WASD to steer, balance, and jump over hazardous traps. On mobile devices, use the touch controls to steer safely.

Game Rules & Goal

Complete each obstacle course without falling off the track. Speed along winding rails, avoid sharp turns, and collect coins to unlock new carts!

Tips & Strategies

  • Control Your Speed: Slow down before sharp turns.
  • Watch for Traps: Pay attention to missing rails and moving obstacles.
